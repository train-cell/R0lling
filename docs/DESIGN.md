# R0lling — Bevel-inspired visual style for existing screens

The numbered references `IMG_0475.png`–`IMG_0490.png` are Bevel screenshots. `IMG_0491.png`–`IMG_0495.png` are Dribbble concepts and `IMG_0496.png`–`IMG_0497.png` are Mobbin references; those last seven are not Bevel targets. `BEVEL_UI_HYPERANALYSIS.md` is explicitly a LoggedIn analysis with LoggedIn data/API examples and placeholder sketches, so it is useful for visual reading only, not as an R0lling feature or data specification. See [the current screenshot audit](AUDIT_VERIFICATION.md).

The styling goal is to bring R0lling's existing screens closer to the visual language of the Bevel references: approximate charcoal canvas and flat slate cards, restrained outlines and shadows, clear SF typography, periwinkle/lime/amber accents, a deeper blue sleep-detail palette, compact metric layouts, and a subtly translucent floating capsule dock. Keep R0lling's own content, readings, routes, and actions. Hex values below are visual estimates from the supplied screenshots, not Bevel source tokens or a pixel-sampling specification; compression, overlays, and display conditions can shift apparent colors. The reference folder's `IMG_0491`–`IMG_0497` are separate Dribbble/Mobbin concepts and are not Bevel targets.

`IMG_0475`–`0477` are Bevel education slides about Strain, Recovery, and Sleep; `IMG_0481` is Stress & Energy; `IMG_0488`–`0489` are biological-age and biomarker screens. They have no corresponding R0lling screen or data contract and are outside this styling-only goal. Their absence remains documented in the capability inventory. R0lling does not fabricate screens or values to resemble them. Source changes do not establish rendered parity.

Today keeps R0lling's own three productivity indicators: heuristic cognitive load, logged focus time, and record-based daily readiness. The warm/lime/periwinkle colors borrow only the ring styling in `IMG_0478`; they do not represent Bevel Strain, Recovery, or Sleep scores, nor physiological measurements.

The existing Today ring group sits on a rounded `bgElevated` slate panel inside its darker outer card, following the inset ring panel in `IMG_0478`. The daily snapshot and explanatory text remain on the outer card; only the surface treatment changes.

The first three existing Share Overview arcs use amber, lime, and violet-to-periwinkle accents in the order shown in `IMG_0479`–`IMG_0480`. This changes arc styling only; R0lling's metric order, readings, and display-only scales remain unchanged.

This is a visual direction for R0lling, not a claim of pixel-level parity. The screenshots show health, fitness, and sleep screens; they do not include a direct equivalent of R0lling's Vault, Studio, or AI screens. Keep R0lling's own content and data contracts, and do not invent wellness readings to fill a visual layout.

## Layout and components

- The existing Health Monitor grid uses the category iconography and visible labels from `IMG_0482`: lungs/RR, heart/RHR, ECG waveform/HRV, droplet/SpO2, thermometer/Temp, and bed/Sleep. The Today preview reuses the waveform, heart, and bed glyphs for its existing HRV, RHR, and Sleep tiles. Spoken metric summaries and HealthKit readings remain unchanged.
- Use the charcoal canvas with flat slate cards, approximately 20–22pt corner radii, one quiet outline, and a restrained drop shadow. `R0llingBevelSurfaceModifier` handles key cards; `R0llingBevelCapsuleModifier` handles pills and quick actions. Avoid stacked outlines and neon glows.
- Keep page titles prominent and sentence-case. Put the key value first, then unit, date/source, and explanatory detail.
- Use separated circular indicators for independent measures, with an inset dark track, a fine outer rim, and a bright, restrained progress arc. Do not combine metrics with different units into a single ring. Mark heuristic values and unavailable readings clearly.
- The main app navigation uses Today, Journal, Fitness, and Health in a subtly translucent floating capsule, plus a separate circular “more” action. Its quiet outline and selected-tab fill remain legible over the screen beneath it. Vault and secondary screens remain reachable through that action.
- Use a stable color and label for each state; never communicate status by color alone.
- New surfaces use Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, and at least 44pt touch targets for icon actions.
- Product routes, encrypted data, permissions, and metric calculations must remain connected to their actual implementations; reference screenshots are not backend specifications.
